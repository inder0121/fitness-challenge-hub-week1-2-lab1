Welcome to the FitSquad Hub!

FitSquad Hub is a community fitness challenge platform where members can join workout quests, track their progress, and stay motivated with their squad.

Members can track workout duration, earn XP reward points, and complete challenges with difficulty tiers ranging from Beginner to Advanced.

What Makes FitSquad Hub Special?

  • Community fitness quests
  • Workout duration tracking
  • XP reward points
  • Beginner to Advanced challenges
  • Squad motivation and accountability
